## Description
Transform your Apple Watch into a personal wellness companion. NeuroCore delivers gentle, rhythmic haptic patterns designed to help you relax, focus, and sleep better.

**HOW IT WORKS**
NeuroCore uses your Apple Watch's Taptic Engine to deliver soothing vibration patterns. Different rhythms and intensities are designed for different goals - energizing patterns to start your day, focusing patterns for deep work, and calming patterns to unwind.

**10 WELLNESS MODES**
- Energy & Social - Start your day with an energizing boost
- Focus & Flow - Enhance concentration for deep work
- Calm & Unwind - Release tension after a long day
- Sleep, Power Nap & Deep Rest - Gentle patterns for better rest
- Recover - Post-workout relaxation

**SMART FEATURES**
- Biometric Adaptive Mode - Automatically adjusts intensity based on your heart rate and HRV
- Smart Recommendations - Suggests the right mode based on time of day
- Scheduled Sessions - Set daily reminders for your wellness routine
- Streak Tracking - Build healthy habits with daily streak motivation
- Session History - Track your progress over time

**CUSTOMIZABLE**
- Adjustable intensity (5-100%)
- Flexible duration (15 min - 2 hours)
- Save your favorites for quick access
- Watch face complications for one-tap access

**PRIVACY FIRST**
All data stays on your device. Health data is only used to personalize your experience and is never shared.

No subscription. No ads. Just wellness.
