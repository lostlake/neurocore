# Privacy Policy for NeuroCore

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Overview

NeuroCore ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

### Health Data
The App may access the following HealthKit data with your permission:
- Heart Rate
- Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
- Resting Heart Rate

This data is used **only** to:
- Calculate your current stress level
- Automatically adjust haptic intensity in Adaptive Mode

### Local Storage
The App stores the following data locally on your device:
- Your preferences (intensity, duration settings)
- Session history
- Favorite modes
- Scheduled sessions
- Streak data

## Data Sharing

**We do not collect, store, or share any of your data.**

- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party SDKs that collect data

All data remains on your Apple Watch and paired iPhone.

## HealthKit

The App integrates with Apple HealthKit solely to read biometric data for the Adaptive Mode feature. This data:
- Is processed entirely on-device
- Is never transmitted off your device
- Is never stored permanently by the App
- Cannot be accessed by us or any third party

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings > Privacy > Health > NeuroCore.

## Data Retention

- Preferences are stored until you delete the App
- Session history is limited to your 50 most recent sessions
- Deleting the App removes all stored data

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
